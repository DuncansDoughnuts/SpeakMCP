A stateful, permission-bounded Alexa+ simulation that coordinates multiple services while making every consequential action inspectable, approvable, and fail-closed.

    Why this is agentic

    One natural-language intent becomes a multi-step plan across calendar, travel/weather, tasks, messages, shopping preview, and local security inspection surfaces. State persists across scenario runs inside the session.

    Why this is safe

    Read-only steps may run immediately. Write or purchase-adjacent steps require explicit scenario-scoped approval. Forbidden actions remain denied even after approval, and untrusted embedded instructions cannot inherit user authority.

    Why this is judgeable

    Every tool decision emits a structured local event. Judges can inspect blocked actions, policy denials, approvals, scopes, execution order, and session memory.
